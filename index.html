Questions people ask

What happens during a workflow audit?

We trace how work enters, moves, and stalls; review the evidence you can safely share; and return a short ranked plan showing what to fix first, what can wait, and what should stay human.

Does an audit automatically lead to an AI project?

No. The best answer may be a clearer process, a feature in a tool you already own, a staff-assisted workflow, or keeping the work human.

Can Buni implement the recommendations?

Yes, when there is a clear, approved scope. I can help test and implement practical workflows while keeping human review and rollback in place.

Do you only build phone agents?

No. A phone or web assistant is one possible solution when missed calls or messages are the documented problem. I also work on follow-up, scheduling, handoffs, repetitive admin, and team training.

Do you need access to all of my systems?

No. Discovery uses the minimum evidence needed, such as a screen share, read-only report, or redacted export. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer records.
