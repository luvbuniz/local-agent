Practice 01 · Data Asset Advisory

Your records may be worth more than you think.

AI developers need real-world business records to train and evaluate their systems. Years of documented operations can be a meaningful asset, whether your company is growing, winding down, or already closed. We help you find out, and we keep the process discreet, documented, and under your control.

Three ways to take part

Operating companies · typically 25+ employees

Data licensing

For companies with strong operational maturity and substantial internal documentation. License SOPs, knowledge bases, CRM metadata, project histories, QA processes, and operational communications, with sensitive content removed, while you keep running the business.

Closed and wind-down companies · typically 50+ employees at peak

Archive sales

For companies that have ceased or are ceasing operations, and the trustees, assignees, and boards responsible for them. Archives that would otherwise be destroyed can become recovered value for the estate or stakeholders.

Operating companies · typically 25+ employees

Workforce video programs

Ongoing paid programs in which employees who choose to take part record real day-to-day work for AI training and evaluation. Participation is voluntary, consent is individual, and clear rules govern what stays out of frame.

Who we work with

  • Owners and executives of operating companiesTypically 25 or more employees with mature documentation
  • Companies winding down or ceasing operations
  • Chapter 7 and Chapter 11 trustees and debtors-in-possession
  • Assignees for the benefit of creditors, receivers, and liquidators
  • Counsel and financial advisors acting for a client
  • Boards and officers of businesses that have closed or been acquired

Data that may have value

Team communications

Slack and Microsoft Teams workspaces, email archives.

Customer and sales records

CRM metadata, account histories, call and meeting notes.

Procedures and knowledge

SOPs, knowledge bases, playbooks, training materials.

Projects and quality

Project histories, QA processes, tickets, incident records.

Engineering and design

CAD and design files, drawings, technical specifications.

Real-world work footage

Video of everyday tasks, recorded by consenting employees.

Value depends on volume, age, structure, your rights to the data, and how much must be removed or de-identified. Not sure what you have? Describe it and we will tell you plainly whether there is likely a market. Some records should not be sold at all, and we will say so.

  1. 01

    Confidential assessment

    Under NDA, we review a high-level inventory your team prepares: systems, date ranges, volumes, and known sensitivities. You get a candid view of likely demand and what would need to be excluded or de-identified.

  2. 02

    Buyer qualification

    We introduce you only to buyers we have pre-qualified, sharing descriptions, never data. You review interest, terms, use restrictions, and the fee the buyer pays us before deciding anything. You pay us nothing.

  3. 03

    Approval and direct transfer

    Nothing moves until the people authorized to approve it, including a trustee or the court where required, sign off in writing. Data goes directly from your organization to the buyer under the signed agreement. Buni is never in the middle of the data.

Confidentiality and responsible handling

Built for situations where trust is the whole job.

Every organization has obligations to employees, customers, and partners, and organizations in transition also answer to creditors and courts. Our process is designed to respect every one of them.

We never touch the data

Buni does not access, hold, store, or view your records. We work only from inventories and descriptions you choose to share.

Written consent, every time

Nothing is shared with any buyer without written sign-off from the people authorized to give it.

Vetted buyers only

Buyers are reviewed for identity, intended use, security practices, and acceptance of limits on use, onward transfer, and retention.

People come first

Personal information about employees, customers, and third parties is identified early and redacted, de-identified, or excluded before any transfer. Video programs require each participant's informed, voluntary consent.

Sellers never pay us

Data Asset Advisory costs your organization nothing. Buni is paid by the buyer through a referral or finder's fee, and we disclose that fee arrangement to you in writing before any introduction.

Discreet, and never alone

We work alongside your counsel and advisors, follow the approvals your situation requires, and never disclose client names or terms without permission.

Buni is not a law firm and does not provide legal, tax, or accounting advice. Requirements vary by jurisdiction and by the data involved; we recommend that your own counsel review every engagement.

Practice 02 · Workflow & Automation

Practical automation that earns its place.

We trace one real customer or job from first contact to payment, find the costly seams, and separate what needs a process fix from what genuinely benefits from automation or AI.

Workflow audits

Map how work enters, moves, and stalls across calls, inboxes, spreadsheets, and handoffs. Quantify the gaps using your numbers and clearly labeled assumptions.

Automation and AI agents

Connect approved steps, reduce re-keying, and organize follow-up. Where it fits, deploy AI agents for intake, scheduling, or internal requests, always with human review and a rollback path.

Team enablement

Help staff use AI well for everyday work while keeping judgment, safety, and sensitive decisions with people.

What an audit delivers

A short, decision-ready plan, not a software pitch.

  • A plain-English map of the current workflow
  • Up to six ranked findings
  • The math behind the biggest gaps
  • A fix-first, fix-later, and don't-fix plan
  • A clear line between your team's work and ours
  • A bounded pilot design with a success measure

How we work

Principles we hold to across both practices.

Process first

Technology comes after the problem, or the asset, is clearly understood.

Evidence, not hype

Assumptions are labeled, the math is shown, and estimates are never presented as promises.

Human oversight

No changes without approval

FAQ

Questions we are often asked

What is Data Asset Advisory?

We help established organizations find out whether their operational records have value to AI-training data buyers and, if they do, connect them with buyers we have vetted, at no cost to your organization. That includes licensing data from operating companies, selling archives from closed or wind-down companies, and paid workforce video programs. Your organization decides what, if anything, is shared, and signs off in writing before anything moves.

Can a company that is still operating take part?

Yes. Buyers are looking for companies with roughly 25 or more employees, strong operational maturity, and substantial documentation such as SOPs, knowledge bases, CRM metadata, project histories, and QA processes. Data is typically licensed with sensitive content removed, and day-to-day operations are not affected.

What are workforce video programs?

Some buyers run ongoing programs that compensate companies when employees record real day-to-day work for AI training and evaluation. Participation must be voluntary for each employee, with informed consent and clear rules about what stays out of frame, such as customer information and screens showing sensitive data.

Does Buni access or hold our data?

No. We work from inventories, descriptions, and metadata your team prepares. Any transfer happens directly between your organization and the approved buyer, under the agreement you sign. Buni never accesses, holds, or views the underlying data.

What about personal and confidential information in the archives?

Email and chat archives often contain personal information about employees, customers, and third parties, as well as material covered by confidentiality obligations. We raise these issues early with your counsel, and redaction, de-identification, or exclusion is agreed before any transfer. Some records may not be appropriate to sell at all, and we will tell you so.

We are in bankruptcy or an assignment for the benefit of creditors. Can we still do this?

Often, yes, but selling estate assets usually requires approval from the trustee, the assignee, or the court, and transfers involving personal information can require additional review. We work alongside the trustee, counsel, and other advisors and follow the process they set.

How are buyers vetted?

Before any introduction, we review a buyer's identity, intended use, security and data-handling practices, and willingness to accept contractual limits on use, onward transfer, and retention. Buyers who do not meet those standards are not introduced.

Who pays Buni?

The buyer does. Sellers never pay Buni anything for Data Asset Advisory: no retainer, no success fee, and no hourly charge. When an engagement goes forward, the buyer pays us a referral or finder's fee, and we disclose that arrangement to you in writing before any introduction so you can weigh it. Workflow & Automation engagements are separate and are priced in a written scope agreed in advance.

Is Buni a law firm?

No. Buni does not provide legal, tax, or accounting advice. We recommend that your own counsel be involved in every data engagement.

What does the Workflow & Automation practice do?

We trace how work moves through your organization, find where it is missed, repeated, or stuck, and recommend the smallest effective fix: a process change, a feature you already own, a staff-assisted step, or a carefully tested automation or AI agent.

Do you need access to our systems for workflow work?

No. Discovery uses the minimum evidence needed, such as a screen share, a read-only report, or a redacted export. Nothing changes in a live system without an agreed scope and your approval. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer records.

Where does Buni work?

Buni is based in Florida and works remotely with organizations across the United States.
