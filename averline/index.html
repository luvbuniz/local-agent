Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before you start.

Does Averline replace my receptionist?+

No. It is positioned for overflow, after-hours calls, routine questions, and consistent intake. Your team remains responsible for work that requires judgment, authorization, or a human relationship.

Can it use my current phone number?+

Often, a carrier or phone system can forward unanswered or after-hours calls. The exact forwarding method must be tested with your current provider before launch.

Will it update my CRM or book appointments?+

Only when that specific connection and workflow have been configured and tested. Averline does not describe an integration as live simply because an API or connector exists.

How quickly can a pilot launch?+

That depends on the workflow and the systems involved. A narrow intake pilot can move faster than a setup involving transfers, scheduling, texting, or business-system updates.

Can customers try it before we launch?+

Yes. The demonstration links above let you experience fictional industry examples without connecting anything to your business.
