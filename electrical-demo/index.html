What this demonstration shows

The basics your team needs for follow-up.

01

Capture caller and property details

Collect an example name, callback number, address, property type, affected area, and safe observations one question at a time.

02

Understand the reason for the call

Hear whether the caller reports loss of power, a tripping breaker, damaged equipment, lighting work, a panel concern, or a planned installation.

03

Identify urgency for team follow-up

Recognize smoke, fire, sparking, burning smells, downed lines, shock, or water near electrical equipment and move safety ahead of intake.

This demo does not diagnose an electrical problem, provide an estimate, book an appointment, update a CRM, send a text, dispatch an electrician, or notify a team. A live workflow would be designed and tested with each business before those capabilities are described as working.
