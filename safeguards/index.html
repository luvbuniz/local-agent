Process and Safeguards

A summary for owners, executives, trustees, assignees, and counsel considering whether to make operational data available to AI-training data buyers through Buni.

Sellers never pay Buni.

There is no retainer, success fee, or hourly charge to your organization for Data Asset Advisory. Buni is paid by the buyer through a referral or finder's fee. We disclose that arrangement to you in writing before any introduction, so you can weigh it.

Our role, and its limits

Buni assesses whether your operational records are likely to have value to AI-training data buyers, introduces you to buyers we have pre-qualified, and coordinates the process with you and your advisors. Three kinds of engagement are common: data licensing by operating companies, archive sales by closed or wind-down companies, and paid workforce video programs.

  • We never access, hold, store, or view your data. We work only from inventories and descriptions you choose to share. Data moves directly from your organization to the buyer.
  • You decide. Nothing is shared with any buyer without written sign-off from the people authorized to give it.
  • We are not a law firm and do not provide legal, tax, or accounting advice. We recommend that your own counsel be involved in every engagement.

Step by step

  1. Introductory call

    A no-obligation conversation about your organization, its systems, and your goals. No data is requested or shared.

  2. NDA and high-level inventory

    Under a mutual NDA, your team prepares a short inventory: systems, date ranges, approximate volumes, known sensitivities, and who holds the rights to the data.

  3. Assessment and readiness

    We give you a candid view of likely demand and flag what would need to be excluded, redacted, or de-identified. For estates and assignments, we identify the approvals your process requires.

  4. Buyer qualification and introduction

    We introduce you only to buyers we have pre-qualified, sharing descriptions, never data. Before any introduction you receive written disclosure of the fee the buyer pays Buni.

  5. Agreement and approval

    You and your counsel negotiate terms directly with the buyer. Nothing proceeds until the authorized parties, including a trustee or the court where required, approve in writing.

  6. Direct transfer

    Data moves directly from your organization to the buyer under the signed agreement, after the agreed redaction or de-identification. Buni is never in the data path.

Safeguards

ConsentWritten sign-off by authorized parties before anything is shared. Trustee or court approval where the situation requires it.
Personal informationPersonal information about employees, customers, and third parties is identified early and redacted, de-identified, or excluded before transfer, consistent with your privacy commitments and applicable law.
Regulated dataHealth, financial-account, children's, and similarly regulated data is excluded unless your counsel confirms a lawful basis.
Third-party confidentialityMaterial covered by customer, vendor, or partner confidentiality obligations is excluded unless those obligations permit the transfer.
Workforce video programsParticipation is voluntary for each employee, with individual informed consent, the right to decline without consequence, and clear rules about what stays out of frame, such as customer information and screens with sensitive data.
Buyer vettingIdentity, intended use, security and data-handling practices, and acceptance of contractual limits on use, onward transfer, retention, and re-identification.
FeesSellers pay nothing. The buyer's fee to Buni is disclosed to you in writing before any introduction.
DiscretionWe do not disclose client names, matters, or terms without permission.

What we will ask you to confirm

  • You have authority to approve the engagement, or know who does.
  • Your organization owns, or has the right to license, the data in question.
  • Any limits in your privacy policy, employee notices, or customer terms.
  • Contracts that restrict how certain records may be used or disclosed.
  • Any regulated or especially sensitive categories in your systems.
  • For estates and assignments: the approval process and who leads it.

What we will not do

  • Access, copy, or store your data, or ask for system credentials.
  • Introduce a buyer who has not been pre-qualified, or share data with anyone.
  • Recommend transferring data your counsel has not cleared.
  • Charge your organization a fee for Data Asset Advisory.
