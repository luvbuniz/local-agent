## What Buni does

Buni designs and configures AI receptionists and website chat assistants for local service businesses. A common starting point is after-hours or overflow support: answer a new inquiry, capture contact details, understand why the person reached out, and identify urgency for a team's follow-up.

Each live workflow is scoped, connected, tested, and approved with the business. Calendar booking, texting, CRM updates, dispatch, payments, review collection, and other integrations must not be described as active until that exact workflow has passed an end-to-end test.

## Typical fit

Buni may be useful for trades and other local service businesses, including roofing, plumbing, HVAC, gutters, electrical work, remodeling, salons, law offices, and insurance agencies. The goal is overflow and after-hours coverage, not replacing a business's employees.
