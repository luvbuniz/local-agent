# Bayline Auto Care - fictional demo knowledge base

Bayline Auto Care is a fictional independent auto-repair shop created only for
Buni's public demonstration. These facts are examples, not claims about a real
business.

## Fictional shop facts

- Location: Bradenton, Florida.
- Example service area: Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, Palmetto, and nearby Manatee
  County communities.
- Example hours: Monday-Friday, 7:30 a.m.-5:30 p.m.; Saturday,
  8 a.m.-2 p.m.; closed Sunday.
- Example services: maintenance, oil changes, brakes, batteries and starting,
  warning-light diagnostics, cooling systems, air conditioning, tires,
  suspension, steering, and general mechanical repair.
- Not offered in this demo: collision/body repair, roadside assistance, towing,
  mobile repair, glass replacement, or emergency response.

## Demo limits

- The demo cannot see or inspect a vehicle.
- The demo cannot determine whether a vehicle is safe to drive.
- The demo cannot diagnose a problem or quote a repair.
- The demo cannot check a live shop schedule, reserve a bay, order a part,
  arrange a tow, take payment, or update a shop-management system.
- All names, phone numbers, addresses, vehicles, and service histories supplied
  by visitors should be fictional.

## Common questions

### Can you tell me what is wrong from the warning light or noise?

No. A trained technician would need to inspect and test the vehicle before the
shop could diagnose it.

### Can I drive it to the shop?

The demo cannot say that a vehicle is safe to drive. Smoke, fire, overheating,
unsafe braking, serious steering trouble, a flashing check-engine light, or a
vehicle stopped in a dangerous location should be treated as a safety concern.

### How much will it cost?

The fictional shop does not publish repair prices in this demo. The vehicle
would need an inspection before a service advisor could provide a responsible
estimate.

### Can you book me for tomorrow?

No live shop calendar is connected. The demo can collect a preferred day or
time for an example follow-up but cannot reserve it.

### Do you tow cars?

The fictional shop does not operate a tow service in this demo. A caller who
cannot safely drive should contact a qualified local roadside or towing
provider.

### ¿Habla español?

Sí. La demostración puede continuar en español y debe mantener las mismas
reglas de seguridad, privacidad y honestidad.
