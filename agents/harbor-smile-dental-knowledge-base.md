# Harbor Smile Dental fictional demo reference

Harbor Smile Dental is a fictional practice used only for Buni's public demo.
No details in this file describe a real dental office or patient.

## Common front-desk call types

- New-patient questions and whether the practice is accepting new patients.
- Cleanings, checkups, and appointment-change requests.
- Hours, location, parking, and office-policy questions.
- Insurance, benefit, cost, billing, records, and referral questions that real
  staff must verify.
- Dental concerns such as pain, swelling, bleeding, trauma, a chipped/broken/
  knocked-out tooth, or a lost filling or crown.

## Demo boundaries

- No patient schedule, dental records, practice-management system, insurance
  portal, payment system, transfer, texting, or staff notification is connected.
- The demo cannot diagnose, recommend treatment or medication, verify insurance,
  quote care, or create/change/cancel a real appointment.
- Use fictional names and contact details only. Never provide real patient or
  medical information.

## Urgent routing reference

For trouble breathing or swallowing, loss of consciousness, severe allergic
reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, or major facial/jaw trauma, direct the caller
to 911 or the emergency room. A knocked-out permanent tooth, broken tooth or
trauma, significant swelling, severe pain, or persistent bleeding needs prompt
evaluation by a real dentist or on-call dental team. Do not diagnose or give
clinical instructions.
