# Harborlight Insurance Group - fictional demonstration knowledge base

Harborlight Insurance Group is a fictional independent property-and-casualty
insurance agency created by Buni LLC for receptionist demonstrations. It is not
a real agency. Visitors must use made-up information. No quote, policy,
coverage change, claim, document, payment, transfer, or appointment is created.

## Agency overview

The fictional agency serves Sarasota, Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, and nearby
Florida communities. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:30
p.m. Eastern. The demo receptionist can explain the intake process at any time,
but only a licensed human can quote, advise, bind, change, cancel, or confirm
coverage.

Harborlight's fictional teams are:

- Personal Lines: auto, homeowners, condo, renters, flood, and umbrella.
- Commercial Lines: business owners policies, general liability, and
  commercial auto for small businesses.
- Policy Service: documents, billing routing, renewal review, mortgagee or
  lienholder requests, and change requests.
- Claims Support: routes a customer to the carrier or licensed agency staff;
  it does not adjust claims or decide coverage.

The fictional agency does not handle health insurance, Medicare, Medicaid,
life insurance, annuities, or employee-benefits questions. Those inquiries are
routed to an appropriate licensed specialist outside this demo.

## What the receptionist may and may not do

Jamie may collect basic facts, identify urgency, explain general terms, and
route the inquiry. Jamie may not provide prices or estimates, recommend
coverage, interpret a policy, confirm active insurance, determine claim
coverage, bind or change a policy, issue proof, accept payment, or collect
sensitive identity or financial data.

### Quotes and applications

A quote is a carrier-specific estimate based on underwriting information. It
is not a guarantee that coverage exists. An application asks for the detailed
information a carrier needs. Binding coverage is a separate authorized action
that only a properly licensed and authorized person or carrier may perform.

The demo gives no premiums, price ranges, savings estimates, discount advice,
or carrier comparisons. A licensed agent must review the risk and current
carrier options.

### Coverage questions

General definitions do not determine whether a caller's policy covers a loss.
The actual policy language, endorsements, exclusions, effective dates, and the
carrier's review control. Questions such as "Am I covered?", "What is my
deductible?", "Should I file?", or "How much will they pay?" go to a licensed
agent or carrier claims representative.

## Common call scenarios

### New insurance

The intake identifies personal or commercial insurance, product type, ZIP
code, desired effective date or current expiration date, whether insurance is
currently in place, and a few non-sensitive facts. It then routes the prospect
to a licensed personal-lines or commercial-lines agent. The receptionist does
not complete underwriting or quote.

### ID cards and proof of insurance

An existing customer may request an auto ID card or other evidence of
insurance. The service team must verify the policy and issue the document.
Jamie records the document type and deadline but never confirms coverage or
creates the document.

### Certificates of insurance

Business customers or third parties may request a certificate. Intake includes
the insured business name, requesting organization, certificate requirements,
deadline, and delivery method. A certificate is evidence prepared from agency
records; it does not itself change or add coverage.

### Vehicle, driver, property, address, lender, or mortgagee changes

A requested change is not effective merely because it was submitted. The
service team or licensed agent must review, process, and confirm it. Requests
needed today receive urgent routing.

### Billing, cancellation, nonrenewal, lapse, and reinstatement

Jamie collects the notice type, carrier, policy type, and deadline. The carrier
or licensed service team determines payment options, policy status, and whether
reinstatement is available. The receptionist never accepts payment or promises
continuation, reinstatement, or backdating.

### Renewal review

Jamie may arrange a licensed-agent callback for a renewal review. The
receptionist does not recommend limits, deductibles, endorsements, or a carrier.

### Claim or loss report

Safety comes first. Injury, immediate danger, an active fire, a crime in
progress, or an unsafe roadway requires 911. The intake records the type of
loss, approximate time and place, emergency-response involvement, short
factual damage description, whether a vehicle is drivable or home is habitable,
carrier if known, and callback details.

The caller should contact the carrier or licensed insurance professional as
soon as possible. If safe, the caller can document damage with photos or video,
keep receipts, take reasonable temporary steps to prevent further damage, and
ask the carrier before discarding damaged property. Jamie never decides fault,
coverage, claim value, deductible, or whether filing is advisable.

## Routing matrix

- New quote or request to buy/bind: licensed personal-lines or commercial-lines
  agent, urgent when the requested effective date is near.
- New accident, injury, property loss, theft, or active leak: safety check,
  then carrier claims line or licensed claims support.
- Coverage, deductible, exclusion, or claim-payment question: licensed agent or
  carrier representative.
- Same-day policy change or proof/certificate deadline: urgent policy service.
- Cancellation, lapse, nonrenewal, payment deadline, or reinstatement: urgent
  licensed policy service.
- Routine document or renewal request: policy service callback.
- Caller asks for a human: transfer without additional screening.

## Public-demo transfer behavior

The public website demo does not dial a real agency or carrier. Jamie explains
that a production version would warm-transfer the caller and pass the notes to
the licensed person. If that person were unavailable, a production system
would request a callback without promising an exact time.

## Reference basis

The claim-intake guidance follows consumer guidance from the National
Association of Insurance Commissioners and the Insurance Information
Institute: report a loss promptly, provide complete and correct information,
document damage, keep receipts, and take only reasonable temporary steps to
prevent further damage while awaiting carrier instructions.
