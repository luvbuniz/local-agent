# Aster & Ash Color House - fictional demonstration knowledge base

Aster & Ash Color House is a fictional high-end hair and color salon created
by Buni LLC for receptionist demonstrations. It is not a real business. Demo
visitors should use made-up contact details; no appointment or payment is
created.

## Salon overview

The fictional salon is in Sarasota, Florida. Hours are Tuesday through
Thursday 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., Friday and Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.,
and Sunday and Monday closed.

The salon offers haircuts, blowouts, event styling, single-process color,
root retouches, glosses, highlights, balayage, gray blending, fashion color,
color correction, and extensions. It does not offer nails, facials, waxing,
injectables, laser treatments, or other med-spa services.

Prices include a consultation, shampoo, conditioning treatment, blow-dry, and

Gratuity is optional and not included.

A complimentary 15-minute color consultation is available for major
transformations, corrections, vivid color, and extensions.

Maya specializes in balayage, lived-in blondes, gray blending, and subtle
high-end transformations. She is the best-fit recommendation for a new guest
wanting natural-looking blonde, soft dimension, or lower-maintenance grow-out.

Jordan specializes in corrective color, vivid/fashion shades, and major

Nina specializes in curls, textured cuts, dimensional brunettes, and

Sofia specializes in precision cuts, bobs, blowouts, and event styling. She

## Color expectations

Previous box dye, henna, metallic salts, or a major lightening request requires
a complimentary consultation and strand test. Vivid shades and color
corrections may take multiple appointments. The salon never guarantees that a
reference-photo result can be completed in one visit.

Guests should arrive with dry hair in its normal condition for a consultation.
Reference photos are welcome in a real deployment. A patch test may be
required at least 48 hours before color for a known sensitivity or when using a
new color line.

## Deposits, changes, and timing

New-guest color appointments require a $75 deposit applied to the service.
Color correction and extension appointments require a 50% deposit after the
consultation. Changes or cancellations require 48 hours' notice. Late
cancellations and no-shows forfeit the deposit.

Guests arriving more than 15 minutes late may need a shortened service or a
new appointment. A production receptionist would offer a waitlist for earlier
openings but would never promise that a slot will become available.

Typical timing:

- Haircut: 60 minutes.
- Blowout: 45 minutes.
- Root retouch: 2 hours.
- All-over color: 2 to 2.5 hours.
- Partial highlights: 2.5 to 3 hours.
- Full highlights or balayage: 3.5 to 4.5 hours.
- Color correction: 3 to 6 hours.

## Safety

Trouble breathing, facial swelling, or a severe active reaction requires 911.
Burning, broken skin, or an active scalp reaction is not appropriate for a
color booking. Poppy does not diagnose conditions or give medical advice.
