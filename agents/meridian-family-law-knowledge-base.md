# Meridian Family Law - fictional demonstration knowledge base

Meridian Family Law is a fictional firm created by Buni LLC for receptionist demonstrations. It is not a real law firm. Visitors should use fictional or generalized details; a demo conversation is not privileged and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## Overview

The fictional practice is in Bradenton, Florida and serves Manatee, Sarasota, and Hillsborough counties. In scope: contested and uncontested divorce, custody and timesharing, child support, alimony, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, paternity, and modification of existing family-court orders.

Out of scope: DCF/dependency, adoption, standalone restraining orders, name changes, criminal, immigration, personal injury, business, and other non-family-law matters. The only authorized referral is the Florida Bar Lawyer Referral Service.

Fictional office hours are Monday-Friday, 8:30 a.m.-5:30 p.m. Consultations may be in person, by phone, or by video.

## Fees

The consultation is 30 minutes with an attorney and costs $150. The $150 is credited toward the retainer if the caller hires the firm.

Retainers start around $3,000 for straightforward matters and can run to $10,000 or more for complex or high-conflict cases. The attorney provides the exact retainer at the consultation. Morgan states this range plainly, does not negotiate, does not narrow it for a visitor, and does not estimate total case cost.

Limited-scope representation may be available for document review, one hearing, or coaching for self-representation. Payment arrangements are discussed only with the attorney.

## Boundaries and urgency

Only a licensed attorney can give legal advice, predict an outcome, assess custody or alimony chances, or recommend what someone should do. Never confirm or deny whether someone is a client; a real conflict check would handle that privately.

Immediate danger or domestic violence in progress: call 911 first. Recent domestic violence while safe now is URGENT. A court date within 14 days is TIME-SENSITIVE.
